WHERE THE 6408 NUMBERS COME FROM
  L, hex, ORB end: Air-Way / SAE J1926 as before - the Brennan -08 and -20 STEPs agree exactly (L 0.800 / 1.200,
  hex 0.875 / 1.875, same thread length and groove).
  Hole diameter: Air-Way HOLE column (Brennan 0.422 at -08, 1.093 at -20 - same).
  Hole depth: Air-Way does not publish it. Brennan: full diameter to 0.020 past the hex face at -08 and 0.040 at -20,
  then a 120 deg drill point. Other sizes are interpolated by dash size (ASSUMPTION, logged per row). The drill point
  is not modelled (flat bottom, as on 2408). With the point, every size would keep 0.23-0.28 in of solid head,
  in line with Brennan's 0.22 (-08) and 0.25 (-20).
  Which OMi SKU is which: OMi sells 6408-04-O and 6408-04-O-SLD ("External Hex Plug Solid"), and Brennan's 6408-08-O /
  6408-20-O are hollow - so the plain SKUs are the hollow plugs. The Air-Way SOLID rows at -06...-32 have no OMi SKU
  and are marked DROP in DesignTable_6408.xlsx (switching one back is a one-cell change).
