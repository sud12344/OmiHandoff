WHAT IS NEW
  The swivel end = a nut (its own hex) sitting over a nose with the 37 deg seat, drawn inside the leg revolve like
  the batch 8 ports: nut face -> countersink -> nut thread (helical, straight) -> relief -> slot around the nose ->
  nose tip -> 37 deg seat -> bore. The nut and the body are ONE solid, as in Brennan's own STEP files.
  Two new operations, and the report lines to watch:
    - Straights: the body hex is no longer cut through the whole part - "HEXCUT created in the right zone"
      (it stops at the body face behind the nut) and "NUTCUT created in the right zone" (the nut hex, cut from
      the nut face). 6565 has three zone cuts (body + both nuts) and "START=" in the HEXCUT dims.
    - Elbows / tees: "NUTHEXn nut flats normal to Z" and "NUTCUTn created and removed material".
    - All: "... sketch fully defined", "Revolved volume ... (expect ...)", "bore reaches the swivel seat",
      "THD_End... helix created / swept cut created".
  6565 is the first part whose End A is not a tip on the axis: the macro puts the origin on the End A nut face
  instead. If its DRIVE sketch is not fully defined, send me that report first.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM (details: 00_DOCS\Swivel_end_Brennan6500_measurements.md, pictures in 05_DIAGRAMS)
