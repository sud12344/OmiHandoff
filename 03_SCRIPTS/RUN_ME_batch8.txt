WHAT IS NEW
  First female ports drawn INSIDE the DRIVE revolve (the port is a cavity in the profile, not a separate cut):
    6404/6410  female SAE ORB port: 15 deg O-ring chamfer, 45 deg lead-in, minor bore, 120 deg drill point
    2406       female 37 deg JIC port: 45 deg countersink, minor bore, 37 deg internal seat
  Report lines to look at:
    - "DRIVE sketch fully defined" and "Revolved volume ... (expect ...)"   <- the cavity profile
    - "dim L32/L33/L23 = ..."  the overall length is now dimensioned between the two end faces
    - "dim ORBFB_... / JICFB_... = ..."  the port dimensions
    - "bore open on the axis at End B"   <- the through bore breaks into the port
    - "THD_EndB helix created / swept cut created"  <- straight internal thread (same code as the 0318 nut)
  If DRIVE is not fully defined or the volume check fails, send me the report - that is the new part.
