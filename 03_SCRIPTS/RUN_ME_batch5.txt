WHAT IS NEW, AND WHAT TO LOOK AT IN THE REPORTS
  - Bulkhead ends (2700/2705/2706): the JIC end with its thread run the full bulkhead length. Same code path as
    the JIC ends you have already built, just a longer thread. -LN configs are the same fitting sold with a
    locknut; the locknut is a separate part (0306) and is not modelled.
  - Beaded hose (4404/4604): new stub + bead end. Look for "HOSEA_... = ..." dimension lines and a clean sweep.
  - 5404N: first straight without a hex - the macro skips the hex cut; the report has a "Body diameter" check
    instead of the hex check.
  - 2503 is the first part with a leg that is NOT on the X or Y axis (the JIC leg sits at 135 deg). New report lines:
       LEGANG2 = 135.00 deg, "LEG2 sketch fully defined", "PLN2 reference plane created", "PLN2 is normal to the
       angled leg", HEX2 extruded the right way, BORE2 / THD_End2 on the angled leg, "leg 2 tip (exact)".
    If anything in 2503 fails, send me the report - it only affects this one macro.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM (every assumption is also in $PRP@AxialSource of each row)
  Bulkheads  Brennan 2705-08-08 STEP: the bulkhead thread IS the 3/4-16 JIC thread, running from the relief to
             the land; its length from the relief to the tip (1.44) = Air-Way I1. So: MS33656J nose (VERIFIED) +
             thread over Air-Way I1 per part. This closes the "bulkhead thread open" item on 2700.
             2700: the bulkhead is the SECOND dash (2700-06-04 I1 1.20 = the -04 bulkhead length).
             Not modelled: Brennan's 0.04 collar at the hex face and its slightly larger relief.
  4404/4604  Tube OD = Air-Way TUBE OD column, bead dia = Air-Way AA, bore = Air-Way D (Brennan 4404-08-06: 0.500 /
             0.560 / 0.406 - all three match). Stub length 1.500, bead position 0.251 from the tip and bead width
             come from that one Brennan STEP (ASSUMED for the other sizes); with them Air-Way's overall length gives
             a 0.29 hex on 4404-08-06 = Brennan exactly. The round bead section is drawn as a flat-topped ring.
  5404N      Air-Way bar diameter C and length; NPTF threads over L2 only (vanish not modelled).
  2503       Brennan 2504-08-08-FG STEP (45-deg JIC elbow): same forged body as the 90-deg elbows - one hex arm per
             leg with its shoulder at M - end length (0.323 on the STEP = 0.98 - 0.657) and the same 30-deg corner
             puck. Checked for every row that the puck never covers the inner thread edge (tightest 2503-06-08-FG,
             0.006 clear - flagged in its $COMMENT).
