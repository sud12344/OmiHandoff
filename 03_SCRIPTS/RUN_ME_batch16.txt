THE CORNER-PUCK FIX (QA #171)
  On 90 / 45 deg elbows the outer corner is filled by a "puck" (two 30 deg cones). Its equator sat exactly on the hex corner radius,
  so its section equalled the hex-arm end face and SolidWorks silently dropped it from the union in 20 configurations - the outer
  corner stayed square, with no error: 2501-06-02-FG, 2501-L-06-04-FG, 2503-06-04-FG, 2701-02-02 / -06-06 / -06-06-LN-FG,
  2702-06-06 / -06-06-LN-FG, 6500-06-04 / -06-06-FG, 6502-06-06-FG, 6801-06-04 / -06-05 / -06-06-NWO-FG, 6802-06-04 / -06-06-NWO-FG
  (16 shipped) + 2504-04-04 / -06-06-FG, 6500-L-04-04 / -L-06-06-FG (new). The generator now puts the puck 0.0005 in inside the hex
  corners (PUCK_INSET in gen_tee.py; a construction margin, not a part dimension). All 20 got their corner back; every other
  configuration of the 17 elbow masters changes by that 0.0005 in sliver only (volume -0.00034 x AF^2 in^3 on 90 deg elbows).
  The dimension-check drawings of these masters (10_DRAWINGS\dimension_check_20261009) were made before the fix: no dimension
  changes, only the 20 corners are now filled.
