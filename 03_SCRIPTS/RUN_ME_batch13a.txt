WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM
  Every new size is measured on the Brennan STEP you downloaded (09_STEP_FILES\BRENNAN_*.stp). Each measuring script
  (03_SCRIPTS\measure) was first run on the STEPs we already had and gives exactly the values used before, then on the
  new ones. Air-Way still sets the lengths, hexes and bores; Brennan sets the swivel nut / port / flange-head shapes.
  Single manufacturer for those shapes -> the rows stay ASSUMED.
  Two small changes you may notice: the ORB port O-ring chamfer is 12 deg at -04/-06 (15 deg from -08 up, as Brennan);
  on the NPSM swivels the skirt front is drawn at the hex flats where Brennan's is 0.001 above Air-Way's rounded hex.
