### 5. 37° flare FEMALE / swivel END standard on ASSIST — finding

- MS33656 (male 37° flared end) is CANCELED (18-Oct-1993), superseded by SAE-AS4395 (Active, DoD-adopted 15-FEB-1995). MS33657 (bulkhead male end) likewise canceled → SAE-AS4396.
- There is NO military (MS/AN/MIL) fitting-end standard on ASSIST for the female/swivel internal 37° seat. ASSIST title searches run: "fitting end" (165 records, all 4 pages reviewed), "swivel, flare" (137 records), "internal flare" (37 records), "fitting end, female" (3 records). The only end-standard hit for the internal (female) 37° flare seat is the DoD-adopted SAE document **SAE-AS1708 "Fitting End, Internal Flare Design Standard"** (Active, adopted 13-FEB-1987, reaffirmed 01-MAY-2025). ASSIST hosts only its 1-page adoption notice; the dimensional content must be purchased from SAE. Related adopted SAE assembly standards using it: SAE-AS4405 through SAE-AS4411 ("Internal Flare" adapters/unions/elbows/tees, all Active, 11-Jan-2011). The refrigeration-type MS35867C "CONNECTION END, INTERNAL FLARE, REFRIGERATION" is canceled (S/S by SAE-J513) and is not the 37° aerospace/JIC seat.
